About This Directory

This directory is maintained by the Orleans County Department of Planning and Development as a public resource for property owners, residents, developers, and planning professionals seeking zoning information across Orleans County's towns and villages. All zoning code links connect directly to the official published source for each municipality, though zoning ordinances are subject to amendment and the linked sources may not always reflect the most recent changes. Zoning determines what uses are permitted on a given parcel, what dimensional standards apply, and what approvals may be required before development or a change of use can occur. If you have questions about a specific property or project, contact the relevant Town or Village Zoning Office or Code Enforcement Officer directly. This site is for general informational purposes only and does not constitute legal advice or an official zoning determination.
